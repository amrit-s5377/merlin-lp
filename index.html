Our Philosophy

Refined restraint,
remarkable comfort

The Merlin Group was founded on a single conviction: that true luxury is not decoration, but presence. Each of our three properties occupies a unique setting that shapes every design decision, every menu, every thread count.

We believe the finest hospitality is the kind you don't notice — an environment so thoughtfully considered that comfort becomes invisible, and what remains is simply you, in a beautiful place, with nothing left to want.
